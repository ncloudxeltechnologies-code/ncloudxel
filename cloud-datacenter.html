What We Measure

Success metrics are defined with each customer based on their environment and business objectives.

Infrastructure utilization

Compute, memory and storage efficiency.

Platform availability

Service health, resilience and recovery readiness.

Migration progress

Workloads migrated, validated and accepted.

Provisioning efficiency

Time and consistency of infrastructure delivery.

Operating cost

Resource spend, waste and optimization opportunities.

Security & governance

Coverage of agreed controls and policy requirements.

Specific baselines, targets and reporting frequency are agreed during project discovery. Outcomes depend on the starting environment and agreed scope.
