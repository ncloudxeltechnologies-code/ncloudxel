SECURITY AND DELIVERY INDICATORS

What We Measure

We agree meaningful indicators with your team and track improvement over time rather than relying on tool counts alone.

⌕

Vulnerability Exposure

Open findings by severity, affected assets, age of findings and remediation status.

⌁

Pipeline Security Coverage

Repositories and pipelines covered by agreed code, dependency, container and IaC checks.

▣

Secrets & Identity Hygiene

Secrets exposure findings, credential rotation status, privileged access and least-privilege gaps.

◈

Cloud Configuration Posture

Misconfiguration findings, policy violations, encryption coverage and public exposure risks.

⏱

Remediation Performance

Time to triage, time to remediate, overdue critical findings and exception ageing.

✓

Governance & Compliance Evidence

Control coverage, evidence freshness, audit-action status and policy adherence.

Measurement matters: targets and reporting intervals are defined with the customer. Tool alerts and automated findings require validation and context; no scanner can guarantee that an environment is completely secure.
