OPERATIONAL INDICATORS THAT MATTER

What We Measure

We select indicators based on your architecture and service objectives, helping teams understand system health and operational performance.

◉

Availability & Uptime

Service availability, uptime trends, health checks and error-budget consumption where defined.

↗

Performance & Latency

Response time, p95/p99 latency, throughput and application or infrastructure bottlenecks.

!

Incident Detection

Alert quality, actionable signal coverage, Mean Time to Detect (MTTD) and alert noise.

⏱

Incident Resolution

Mean Time to Acknowledge (MTTA), Mean Time to Resolve (MTTR) and recurring incidents.

▤

Resource & Capacity

CPU, memory, disk, network utilization, saturation and capacity headroom.

✓

Service Reliability

SLIs, SLOs, SLA attainment, error rates, deployment health and resilience indicators.

Measurement matters: baselines, targets and reporting intervals are agreed with the customer. Metrics are interpreted in the context of the application architecture and business impact.
