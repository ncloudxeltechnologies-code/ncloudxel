OUTCOMES THAT MATTER

What We Measure

We use relevant cost, usage and operational indicators to help you understand where money goes and whether changes deliver the intended result.

$

Cloud Spend

Monthly spend, trends and cost by service, account, environment or workload.

↗

Budget Variance

Actual versus planned expenditure, forecast movement and unexpected increases.

◉

Resource Utilization

Compute and database utilization, idle capacity and sizing opportunities.

▤

Storage Efficiency

Storage growth, unattached volumes, snapshots and lifecycle opportunities.

⇄

Network Costs

Data transfer, NAT, cross-zone traffic and related network charges.

✓

Verified Savings

Validated cost changes against an agreed baseline, accounting for usage changes.

Measurement matters: estimates are identified as estimates. Verified savings are reported only after implementation and comparison against an agreed baseline, with relevant usage and pricing changes considered.
